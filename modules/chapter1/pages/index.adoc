*Validation is not a technical exercise—it's a business imperative.*

Before diving into validation techniques, we must understand why validation matters for business applications and how to align validation efforts with business goals.

== Why Validation Matters for Business

Model validation answers critical business questions:

* **Will this model work for our customers?** (Application fit)
* **Can we trust this model in production?** (Risk management)
* **Does this model meet our quality standards?** (Compliance)
* **Is this model worth the deployment cost?** (ROI)

=== The Cost of Skipping Validation

Organizations that skip proper validation face:

* **Production Failures:** Models that work in testing but fail with real users
* **Customer Impact:** Poor quality responses damage brand reputation
* **Wasted Investment:** Deploying models that don't deliver business value
* **Compliance Risk:** Models that violate regulatory requirements

=== The Value of Proper Validation

Organizations that validate properly achieve:

* **Confidence:** Deploy models knowing they meet business requirements
* **Cost Savings:** Avoid deploying unsuitable models
* **Risk Mitigation:** Catch issues before they impact customers
* **Better Decisions:** Data-driven model selection and deployment

== Business Requirements vs. Technical Metrics

=== General Benchmarks Don't Tell the Full Story

Standard benchmarks (MMLU, ARC, HellaSwag) measure general model capabilities but don't answer:

* Does the model understand our industry terminology?
* Can it handle our specific use cases?
* Does it meet our performance requirements?
* Is it safe for our domain?

=== Application-Specific Validation

Application-specific validation tests:

* **Domain Knowledge:** Understanding of your industry and terminology
* **Use Case Fit:** Performance on your specific workflows
* **Performance Requirements:** Latency and throughput for your application
* **Quality Standards:** Accuracy and safety thresholds for your use case

== The Validation Framework

This course uses a three-dimensional validation framework:

=== 1. Accuracy Validation

**Business Question:** "Does the model produce correct answers?"

* Multi-language accuracy (for global applications)
* Domain-specific accuracy (for industry applications)
* Task-specific accuracy (for use case applications)

=== 2. Performance Validation

**Business Question:** "Can the model serve requests fast enough?"

* Latency (user experience)
* Throughput (cost efficiency)
* Resource utilization (scalability)

=== 3. Domain Validation

**Business Question:** "Does the model understand our business context?"

* Terminology understanding
* Workflow handling
* Compliance requirements
* Safety standards
