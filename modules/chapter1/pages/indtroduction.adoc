You've deployed a model. You've tuned it for performance. But the critical question remains: **"Does this model work for MY business application?"**

This course teaches you to answer that question systematically by validating models against real-world business requirements and deploying validation services that ensure ongoing quality.

== The Business Value of Model Validation

Model validation is not just about technical metrics—it's about **business outcomes**:

* **Risk Mitigation:** Catch model failures before they impact customers
* **Cost Optimization:** Deploy only models that deliver value for your use case
* **Compliance:** Ensure models meet regulatory requirements for your industry
* **Confidence:** Make data-driven decisions about model deployment and updates

=== The Validation Challenge

A model that performs well on general benchmarks may fail on your specific use case:

* **Domain Knowledge:** Does it understand your industry terminology?
* **Application Context:** Can it handle your specific workflows?
* **Performance Requirements:** Does it meet your latency and throughput needs?
* **Quality Standards:** Does it meet your accuracy and safety thresholds?

== Course Structure

This course is organized into three modules:

=== Module 1: Understanding Business Value

Learn why validation matters for business applications and how to define validation criteria based on business requirements.

**What you'll learn:**
* How to translate business requirements into validation criteria
* The difference between general benchmarks and application-specific validation
* How to measure ROI of model validation efforts

=== Module 2: Application-Specific Validation

Hands-on labs for validating models across multiple dimensions relevant to business applications:

* **Multi-Language Validation:** Ensure models work across global markets
* **Performance Validation:** Verify models meet production performance requirements
* **Domain-Specific Validation:** Test models on business-relevant scenarios

**What you'll learn:**
* How to create custom validation datasets for your application
* How to run comprehensive validation tests using TrustyAI and GuideLLM
* How to interpret validation results in business context

=== Module 3: Deploying Validation Services

Deploy automated validation services in OpenShift AI that continuously monitor model quality.

**What you'll learn:**
* How to deploy validation services as Kubernetes workloads
* How to automate validation pipelines
* How to integrate validation into your deployment workflow

== Prerequisites

Before starting this course, ensure you have:

* Completed the **rhoai3-deploy** course (model is deployed as an InferenceService)
* Access to an OpenShift AI 3.0+ cluster
* The `oc` CLI tool installed and configured
* Basic understanding of Kubernetes and OpenShift
* Familiarity with model evaluation concepts

[IMPORTANT]
.Verify Your Environment
====
Before proceeding, verify your model is deployed and ready:

[source,bash]
----
# Check your InferenceService status
oc get inferenceservice -n <your-namespace>

# Verify the service is ready
oc get inferenceservice <your-model-name> -n <your-namespace> \
  -o jsonpath='{.status.conditions[?(@.type=="Ready")].status}'
----

The output should be `True`. If not, complete the rhoai3-deploy course first.
====
